## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: MVP home cards
The first-wave Subh home SHALL contain the MVP cards in this order: Tomorrow Morning, Next 10 Mornings, then Weekly Fajrcast.

#### Scenario: Home liquid glass avoids haze-producing frost boosts
- **GIVEN** the Subh home renders liquid-glass cards or controls such as the hero wake-mode selector, Next 10 Mornings, Weekly Fajrcast, or settings
- **WHEN** those surfaces appear on the home screen
- **THEN** they SHALL use the shared home liquid-glass surface treatment
- **AND** that treatment SHALL NOT increase apparent diffusion through blanket tint, stroke, overlay, or fallback-material boosts that create a gray haze
- **AND** any future increase to diffusion SHALL use a direct public glass-thickness or equivalent behind-glass diffusion control when available
- **AND** non-home settings and detail grouped surfaces SHALL NOT be restyled by home-specific glass tuning
