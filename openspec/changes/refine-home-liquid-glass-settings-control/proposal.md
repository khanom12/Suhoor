## Summary

Refine the Subh home liquid-glass treatment by moving the settings control to the top right, restyling it with the same home glass family as the hero selector and forecast cards, and increasing the home surface frost/diffusion slightly.

## Problem

The settings control currently sits in the bottom-right safe-area inset and uses a bespoke circular material treatment. That makes it feel separate from the home surface system used by the hero quick wake selector, Next 10 Mornings, and Weekly Fajrcast cards.

The home glass surfaces also read a little clearer and less diffused than the iOS Weather-style glass target. The app needs a permanent shared home glass setting so future home cards and controls inherit the same treatment without per-component tuning.

## Scope

- Move the home settings control from the bottom right to the top right.
- Restyle the settings control with the shared home liquid-glass surface.
- Add a dedicated home glass surface variant with a modest frost/diffusion increase for home buttons and cards.
- Apply that shared home variant to the hero quick wake selector, Next 10 Mornings card, Weekly Fajrcast card, and settings control.

## Non-Goals

- Do not change home card order, content, copy, data resolution, navigation destinations, or interactions.
- Do not change prayer-time calculation, scheduling, persistence, alarms, or morning resolution.
- Do not restyle settings sheets, detail screens, or non-home grouped surfaces.
