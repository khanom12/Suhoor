## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Morning Hero Quick Wake Mode Transitions

The Morning Hero SHALL animate Fast, Fajr, and Quiet quick wake-state changes without moving the hero stack or rebuilding rows directionally.

#### Scenario: Selector matches forecast-card glass

- **WHEN** the quick wake-state selector is visible
- **THEN** the outer selector pill SHALL use the same grouped translucent glass surface treatment as the Weekly Fajrcast and Next 10 Mornings cards
- **AND** it SHALL preserve the selector's pill shape, selected segment, labels, accessibility, sizing, and interaction behavior.
