## Summary

Move the Weekly Fajrcast card below the Next 10 Mornings card on the Subh home screen.

## Problem

The home surface currently renders Weekly Fajrcast before Next 10 Mornings. For this minor layout adjustment, the forecast list should appear first so the user sees the upcoming morning run before the supporting weekly chart.

## Scope

This change is limited to the vertical order of existing home cards.

## Non-Goals

- Do not change card styling, copy, spacing, data resolution, navigation, or interactions.
- Do not change prayer-time calculation, scheduling, persistence, alarm delivery, or home snapshot generation.
- Do not introduce a new home section, feature flag, or alternate layout.
