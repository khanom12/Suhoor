# Marketing Psychology

Source methodology: https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills/tree/main/skills/marketing-psychology

Read `AGENTS.md` and `.agents/product-marketing.md` first. Psychology advises; it does not dictate.

Prefer models that improve understanding and reduce friction: Jobs to Be Done, first principles, inversion, Occam's Razor, theory of constraints, status-quo bias, paradox of choice, activation energy, Hick's Law, Fogg Behavior Model, EAST, COM-B, framing, and legitimate social proof.

For each recommendation, state the behavioral principle, expected benefit, and ethical risk. Reject tactics that depend on deception, coercion, manufactured anxiety, false scarcity, shame, addiction, or exploiting religious identity.

Fajr Now must never weaponize fear of missing prayer, spiritual guilt, community belonging, or religious authority to force conversion. Behavioral design should make the right action clearer and easier while preserving agency, dignity, and informed choice.
