# Product Marketing Context

Source methodology: https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills/tree/main/skills/product-marketing

Before marketing, copy, CRO, or design work, read `AGENTS.md` and `.agents/product-marketing.md`.

If `.agents/product-marketing.md` does not exist, create it from repo evidence and confirmed project decisions. Separate facts from hypotheses and unknowns. Never invent users, metrics, testimonials, religious claims, pricing, or product capabilities.

Maintain these sections when relevant: Product Overview; Target Audience; Jobs to Be Done; Problems and Pain Points; Competitive Landscape; Differentiation; Objections; Switching Dynamics (push/pull/habit/anxiety); Customer Language; Brand Voice; Proof Points; Goals.

For Fajr Now, `AGENTS.md` is authoritative when generic marketing advice conflicts with product doctrine. Keep the context versioned with a dated changelog. Downstream skills must consume this document rather than recreating positioning from scratch.
