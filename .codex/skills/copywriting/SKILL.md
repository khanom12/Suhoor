# Copywriting

Source methodology: https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills/tree/main/skills/copywriting

Read `AGENTS.md` and `.agents/product-marketing.md` before writing.

Principles: clarity over cleverness; benefits over features; specificity over vagueness; customer language over company language; one idea per section; active/plain language; credible claims over hype. Establish one primary page job and one primary action before drafting.

For landing pages, make the hero immediately answer what this is, who it is for, why it matters, and what to do next. Build a coherent argument down the page rather than a pile of feature cards. Connect each capability to a meaningful morning outcome.

Fajr Now guardrails: calm, dignified, purposeful, trustworthy. No guilt-heavy appeals, fear of religious failure, fake urgency, inflated claims, juvenile gamification language, generic productivity jargon, or invented proof. Do not present the product as religious authority. Do not promise guaranteed waking.

After drafting, always hand the result to `copy-editing` before considering it final.
