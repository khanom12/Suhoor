# Conversion Rate Optimization

Source methodology: https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills/tree/main/skills/cro

Read `AGENTS.md` and `.agents/product-marketing.md` first.

Audit in impact order: value proposition clarity; headline effectiveness; primary CTA and hierarchy; visual hierarchy/scannability; trust and proof; objection handling; friction; mobile and accessibility implications.

Output findings as: quick wins, high-impact changes, hypotheses/tests, and copy alternatives when useful. Treat unmeasured recommendations as hypotheses, not guaranteed lifts.

Fajr Now constraints override generic CRO playbooks. Optimize for qualified understanding, trust, and appropriate action—not clicks at any cost. No fake countdowns, false scarcity, manipulative defaults, guilt, dark patterns, invented social proof, or hiding limitations. Do not sacrifice calmness or truthfulness to increase urgency.
