# Customer Research

Source methodology: https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills/tree/main/skills/customer-research

Read `AGENTS.md` and `.agents/product-marketing.md` first.

Use evidence in this priority order: first-party interviews/surveys/support > observed product behavior > public community/review research > clearly labeled hypotheses. Extract functional, emotional, and social jobs; pain points; trigger events; desired outcomes; alternatives; objections; and verbatim customer language.

Label confidence. High confidence requires repeated independent evidence; medium requires limited/repeated evidence; low means hypothesis or single-source signal. Do not manufacture personas, quotes, consensus, statistics, or religious motivations. Avoid treating practicing Muslims as a monolith.

For Fajr Now, research should sharpen understanding of the Muslim morning and waking for Fajr without exploiting shame, fear, inconsistency, or religious guilt. Feed validated language and insights back into `.agents/product-marketing.md` before copywriting.
